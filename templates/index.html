🌍 About AgroPredict

Agriculture is one of the most important sectors that supports human life. Farmers face challenges in selecting suitable crops, using correct fertilizers, and identifying plant diseases.

AgroPredict is an Artificial Intelligence based smart agriculture system developed to help farmers make better decisions using Machine Learning and Deep Learning technologies.

The system analyzes soil conditions, climate parameters, and plant images to provide accurate recommendations.

Our Objectives

  • 🌾 Crop Recommendation
  • 🌿 Fertilizer Recommendation
  • 🍃 Plant Disease Detection
  • 💊 Treatment Suggestions

🌾 Crop Recommendation
